\section{Large Language Diffusion Models (100 Points)}

\subsection{2.1 Theoretical Questions (30 Points)}

\subsubsection*{2.1.1 Autoregressive vs. Masked Iterative Generation}
\textbf{Autoregressive (AR):} Generates text one token at a time, strictly from left to right ($P(x_t | x_{<t})$). \\
\textbf{Masked Iterative Generation:} Generates the entire sequence at once (parallel) starting from all masks, and iteratively refines/fills in the tokens over multiple steps.

\subsubsection*{2.1.2 Forward Masking as Noise}
% Explain how masking tokens is analogous to adding noise in diffusion.
In continuous diffusion (images), noise is added by perturbing pixel values (Gaussian noise). In discrete text diffusion (LLaDA), "noise" is added by replacing a valid token with a special \texttt{[MASK]} token. As $t$ increases (forward process), the probability of a token being masked increases, destroying information.

\subsubsection*{2.1.3 Reweighting Loss}
% Why do we reweight the loss based on p_mask?
We reweight the loss because the difficulty of the task varies with the noise level. When $p_{mask}$ is low (few masks), the task is easy. When $p_{mask}$ is high (mostly masks), the task is hard and the signal is sparse. Reweighting ensures that all noise levels contribute effectively to the gradient updates without one dominating the other.
