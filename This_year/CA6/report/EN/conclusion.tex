This work implemented and evaluated a feature-based template-to-photo alignment and overlay system grounded in established multi-view geometry and robust estimation. The pipeline produces reproducible overlays, quantitative alignment metrics, and runtime profiles, enabling scientific assessment of both geometric correctness and operational feasibility. The results section demonstrates how qualitative overlays, error distributions, ablation stability, and failure-case diagnostics together characterize performance and boundary conditions. Future extensions should focus on improving robustness under glare and clutter (e.g., ROI gating or hybrid pipelines) and addressing non-planar targets where a single homography is insufficient.
